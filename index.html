Methods & limitations

Framework. Steps: prepare images → pretrained model finds the tumor → user accepts or flags the outline → measure → compare scans → classify → report. The model step is simulated in this prototype.

Measurement. For each enhancing lesion: d1 = longest diameter, d2 = longest diameter at a right angle to d1 (on the slice where d1 × d2 is largest). A lesion is measurable if d1 and d2 are both ≥ 10 mm (the "seen on 2 or more slices" check is done on the 3D mask in the final system). SPD = sum of d1 × d2 of up to 3 measurable target lesions. Change (%) = (new SPD − reference SPD) ÷ reference SPD × 100.

Rules, checked in this order. No enhancing tumor left → Complete response*. Tumor present but no measurable lesion → Non-measurable (not classified by size). SPD up ≥ 25% vs nadir → Progression*. SPD down ≥ 50% vs baseline → Response*. Otherwise → Stable. Baseline = first usable scan. Nadir = lowest SPD so far, including the baseline (non-measurable scans skipped). If the nadir is 0, the % change is undefined and the scan needs expert review. Flagged scans are left out.

* Confirmation. RANO requires a response to be confirmed on a later scan at least 4 weeks apart, and RANO 2.0 recommends confirming progression with a repeat MRI when pseudoprogression is likely. These results are marked "confirm on next scan".

Volume (from the 3D mask) is shown for information only; it is not used for classification, because RANO 2.0 uses different thresholds for volume.

Limitations. Simplified RANO: new lesions, non-enhancing tumor, steroid dose, clinical condition and timing after radiotherapy are not considered. Adult glioma only. Not a medical device.

References: Wen et al. (2023), RANO 2.0, doi:10.1200/JCO.23.01059; Wen et al. (2010), RANO, doi:10.1200/JCO.2009.26.3541. Based on the TumorTrack proposal (v3).
